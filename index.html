Background

3rd place at the APRU × Google Tech Policy Hackathon

Agri-Accept began as AgriAccess, built by a Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) student team for the APRU × Google Tech Policy Hackathon 2025 finals at Google Bangkok.

From 299 participants across 40 universities and 15 economies, 12 teams reached the finals. We placed third overall in the Agricultural Finance & Credit Systems track — recognized for turning NASA-grade satellite signals into bank-readable risk parameters for Indonesian smallholders.

The brief was tech policy with real inclusion stakes: close digital and credit gaps without replacing the lender. Our answer keeps approve / review / reject with the bank, and delivers the missing farm evidence file. Read the official write-up on APRU’s announcement and the finalist project abstracts.

The gap

Loan readiness assistant — not an AI lender

Most Indonesian smallholders lack the 3–5 years of formal paperwork banks require. Without collateral or SLIK history, farms stay invisible to formal credit — and informal rates can climb toward 26%.

Agri-Accept removes the blind spot: independent proof from Earth observation and weather, cash-flow coverage signals, Basel III risk parameters, and a clear fix list officers can discuss with farmers.

Banks and cooperatives keep the credit decision. We deliver the verified evidence pack.

Workflow

How it works

From farm coordinates to a lender-ready evidence pack — explainable end to end.

Locate the farm

Pick an Indonesian demo farm or enter coordinates, crop, and loan details.

Verify with EO + weather

Pull satellite and weather signals for vegetation health, climate stress, and operating risk.

Score with ensemble ML

Random Forest + HistGBM (+ XGBoost) produce credit score, SLIK band, and Basel III parameters.

Explain & decide

SHAP drivers and bilingual GenAI narratives help officers talk with farmers — the bank still decides.

Platform

What the pilot delivers

Built for banking conversations in Indonesia — not a black-box decline letter.

Satellite risk intelligence

Multi-source imagery and NASA Prithvi-style EO features for vegetation and land context at the plot.

Basel III outputs

PD, LGD, EAD, and Expected Credit Loss formatted for risk and credit committees.

Explainable ensemble

RF + HistGBM + XGBoost with SHAP drivers so every score has a readable story.

Indonesian SLIK mapping

Scores aligned to the 1–5 collectibility language used in local supervision.

HKUST GenAI narratives

Bilingual farmer and loan-officer explanations via HKUST GenAI, with Gemini fallback.
