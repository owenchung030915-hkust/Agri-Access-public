How it works

From farm coordinates to a lender-ready evidence pack.

1

Locate the farm

Pick an Indonesian demo farm or enter coordinates, crop, and loan details.

2

Verify with EO + weather

Pull satellite and weather signals to assess vegetation, climate, and operating risk.

3

Score with ensemble ML

Random Forest + HistGBM (+ XGBoost) output credit score, SLIK, and Basel III parameters.

4

Explain & decide

SHAP drivers and GenAI narratives help loan officers talk with farmers.

Platform capabilities
